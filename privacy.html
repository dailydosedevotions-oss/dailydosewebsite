Privacy

Your privacy matters.

A simple guide to the information you share with Daily Dose.

Privacy policy · Updated 22 November 2026

The short version

  • Sign up to receive Daily Dose devotions and related updates by email. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Private prayer requests are not posted on the prayer wall. Requests intended for the public wall are reviewed before appearing.
  • Optional Google Analytics only runs if you accept it. You can change your mind using Privacy choices at the bottom of this page.
  • Have a question about your information? You can contact me directly using the email below.

Daily Dose is run personally by Shaun Loughlin, who is responsible for how your personal information is handled (the data controller).

dailydosedevotions@gmail.com

The details, when you need them

Open a section below to find out more.

Information you choose to share

Daily Dose may receive your name, email address, subscription request, prayer request, answered prayer, or message when you submit a form.

Prayer requests

Private prayer requests are sent to Daily Dose and are not displayed publicly. Public prayer requests may be reviewed before appearing on the prayer wall.

Email

If you subscribe, your email may be used to send Daily Dose devotions and related updates. You can unsubscribe at any time through the email service.

App install stats

The site records simple aggregate PWA/app activity, such as install signals and app opens. These stats do not collect names, email addresses, or personal identities.

Optional website analytics

With your consent, Daily Dose Devotions uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how people find and use the website. This may measure pages viewed, visits, approximate location such as country, device type, referral source, campaign parameters, engagement, and outbound link clicks through GA4 Enhanced Measurement.

Analytics is disabled by default. Google’s analytics script is not requested unless you select Accept analytics. Advertising storage, ad user data, and ad personalisation remain denied. The lawful basis for this processing is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the applicable Irish ePrivacy rules.

You can reject analytics without losing access to any essential site feature. You can also change or withdraw your choice at any time by selecting Privacy choices at the bottom of the page. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before consent was withdrawn.

Google may process analytics information on servers outside Ireland or the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, Google states that it uses recognised transfer safeguards, which may include adequacy decisions and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google’s privacy policy for more information.

Daily Dose will configure the GA4 data-retention setting to retain event-level user data for no longer than 14 months. Aggregated reporting may remain available for longer.

Consent record

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember it. This preference is separate from the site’s aggregate PWA/app activity counter.

The services that help run Daily Dose

Cloudflare helps host and operate the website. Brevo handles email subscriptions and email delivery. Messages sent to the Daily Dose Gmail address are handled through Google. Optional website analytics uses Google Analytics, as explained above.

These services process information needed to provide their services. A private prayer request is not published on the website, but sending it by email involves the email services used to deliver and receive it.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, request deletion, or ask for its use to be restricted. Depending on the circumstances, you may also have the right to object to processing or receive your information in a portable format.

Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it. This does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal. Use the unsubscribe link in an email to stop subscription emails, or use Privacy choices to change your analytics choice.

To make a privacy request, email dailydosedevotions@gmail.com. You can also raise a concern with Ireland’s Data Protection Commission.

A note about prayer requests

Please share only what is needed for your request. Avoid including someone else’s identifying details or sensitive information without their permission. Anything approved for the public prayer wall can be read and shared by others.
