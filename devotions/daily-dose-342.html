The Work God Finishes

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 1, 2027.

PHILIPPIANS 1:6 (ESV)

“And I am sure of this, that he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.”

Some changes happen quickly. Others take years of correction, surrender and patient grace. That can be frustrating when you can clearly see how far you still have to go.

Paul’s confidence was not placed in the strength of the people he was writing to. It was placed in God. The Lord had begun the work, and the Lord would remain faithful to complete it. Following Jesus is not God handing us a new life and then leaving us to finish it alone. From beginning to end, we depend upon His grace—His undeserved help and kindness toward us.

This does not make us passive. We still repent, obey, pray and learn. But we do those things with hope rather than despair. A slow season does not mean God has stopped working. A struggle does not mean He has abandoned you. Even conviction is evidence of His mercy, because He is bringing hidden things into the light so they can be changed.

Do not measure God’s faithfulness only by how finished you feel today. Look back and notice where He has already softened your heart, corrected your direction or taught you to depend upon Christ.

Thank Him for what has changed. Trust Him with what remains.

Remember: You are unfinished, but you are not forgotten.
