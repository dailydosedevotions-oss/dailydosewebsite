Life in the Spirit, Part 1: The Helper With You

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 14, 2027.

JOHN 14:16–17 (ESV)

“He will give you another Helper, to be with you forever.”

The night before the cross, Jesus told His disciples He was going away. They were confused and afraid. Into that fear, He promised “another Helper”—the Holy Spirit.

The Spirit is not a vague energy or a religious feeling. He is the third Person of the Trinity: fully God, personal and holy. Jesus said the Spirit would live with and in His people.

Christians use the word “Trinity” to describe the Bible’s teaching that there is one God who eternally exists as Father, Son and Holy Spirit. This is not three gods, and the Spirit is not a lesser assistant. The truth is greater than our ability to explain fully, but Scripture gives us enough to reject the idea that the Spirit is merely a power we control.

“Helper” carries the sense of one who comes alongside—comforting, teaching, reminding, convicting and strengthening. The Spirit does not replace Jesus or draw attention away from Him. He makes the truth of Christ known and forms Christlike life in believers.

This promise matters when God feels distant. Christians do not depend upon a mood to prove His presence. Feelings change. Jesus promised that the Spirit would remain.

If you are exploring Christianity, the idea may be unfamiliar. Begin with the words of Jesus rather than popular claims or strange impressions. The Holy Spirit will never contradict the Christ revealed in Scripture.

If you belong to Jesus, you are not left to live the Christian life alone. God has made His home with His people.
