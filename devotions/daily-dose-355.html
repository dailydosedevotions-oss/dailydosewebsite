What the Lord Requires

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 14, 2027.

MICAH 6:8 (ESV)

“To do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God.”

The people in Micah’s day wanted to know what religious offering would satisfy God. Micah’s answer cut through performance. God wanted lives shaped by justice, mercy and humility.

To do justice is to choose what is right rather than what is merely convenient. To love kindness is to value mercy, not offer it reluctantly. To walk humbly with God is to live without pretending we are self-made, self-saving or above correction.

These words are simple to remember and difficult to practise. Justice may require speaking when silence would be easier. Kindness may cost time. Humility may mean admitting we were wrong.

Micah was not teaching that good behaviour earns salvation. The Bible’s larger story shows that we need God’s mercy because none of us has lived perfectly. But those who receive mercy are called to reflect God’s character in the way they treat others.

Choose one concrete response today: correct something unfair, show mercy where you could be harsh, or admit where pride has taken over.

Faith becomes visible in ordinary choices.
