Eager to Examine

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on January 10, 2027.

ACTS 17:11 (ESV)

"They received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so."

The Bereans show us a beautiful balance: eagerness and examination. ACTS 17:11 (ESV) says they received the word with all eagerness, while examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so. They were not cynical, but they were not careless either.

Some people are eager but undiscerning. They receive anything that sounds spiritual, emotional, impressive, or popular. Others examine everything with a cold suspicion that has little love for truth. The Bereans teach a better way. They welcomed the Word, and they tested what they heard by Scripture.

This is vital because not every religious voice is faithful. Not every confident speaker is accurate. Not every moving statement is biblical. The believer must not be shaped by personality, trend, or emotion more than by the written Word of God.

Daily examination also shows hunger. They did not check Scripture once and move on. They searched daily. That kind of attention honours God. ISAIAH 8:20 (ESV) says, "To the teaching and to the testimony!" God's people are not left to drift wherever voices lead. They have His Word.

Be eager for truth, but do not be lazy with discernment. Open Scripture. Test what you hear. Ask whether the message exalts Christ, agrees with the whole counsel of God, and leads toward obedience. A mature believer is not easily impressed by spiritual sound. They want what is true.

This also protects the church from personality-led faith. A gifted voice can be helpful, but no teacher is above Scripture. A moving message can encourage, but emotion is not the test of truth. The Berean spirit honours God because it receives teaching with hunger and humility while giving final authority to the Word. That kind of discernment is not suspicion for its own sake. It is love for truth, love for Christ, and care for the soul.
