Fear Not, I Am With You

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 16, 2027.

ISAIAH 41:10 (ESV)

"Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God."

God does not comfort His people with empty optimism. ISAIAH 41:10 (ESV) says, "Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God." The reason not to fear is not that life is easy. The reason is His presence.

In context, the Lord is speaking to His chosen servant Israel and assuring them of His covenant presence among hostile nations. Christians should not detach the words from that setting, yet the passage truly reveals the faithful character of the God who does not abandon His people.

The verse continues with promises: "I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand." God does not merely tell His people to be brave. He gives Himself as their strength, help, and upholding.

Fear often asks, "What if I cannot carry this?" The Lord answers, "I will uphold you." Fear asks, "What if I am alone?" The Lord answers, "I am with you." Faith does not ignore the questions; it listens to the greater answer of God.

DEUTERONOMY 31:6 (ESV) says, "He will not leave you or forsake you." The presence of God has always been the courage of His people.

Let this steady you today. You may not know how every detail will work out. You may still feel weakness. But the Lord does not abandon His own. Fear not, not because you are strong in yourself, but because He is your God.

The words "I am with you" are enough because of who speaks them. If a weak helper says it, comfort is limited. When the Lord says it, everything changes. His presence is not symbolic. He strengthens, helps, and upholds. Bring today's fear under that promise. You are not being asked to face life without Him.

This promise does not rest on your ability to feel brave. It rests on God's covenant faithfulness. He says, "I am your God." That is the ground beneath trembling feet.

Stand there today.
