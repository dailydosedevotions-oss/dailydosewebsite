Grace Is Sufficient

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 25, 2027.

2 CORINTHIANS 12:9 (ESV)

“My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.”

Paul asked God three times to remove what he called a “thorn in the flesh.” God answered, but not by removing it. He promised sufficient grace.

That answer is not easy. We often want God to prove His care by changing the circumstance. Sometimes He does. At other times, He gives strength to endure what remains.

Grace is God’s undeserved favour and active help. “Sufficient” does not mean the weakness feels pleasant or stops mattering. It means weakness does not place us beyond the reach of Christ’s power.

Paul learned that his limitations could keep him from building life on self-confidence. The place he wanted hidden became a place where the strength of Jesus was seen.

This verse should never be used to dismiss another person’s pain or keep them from seeking appropriate help. Paul prayed honestly and repeatedly. We can do the same. Ask for healing, support and change. But do not assume God is absent if the answer takes a different form.

Bring Him what feels insufficient: energy, courage, ability or endurance. You do not need to pretend it is enough.

Your weakness is real. His grace is greater.
