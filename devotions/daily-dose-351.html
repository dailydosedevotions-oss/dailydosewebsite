Abide in Christ, Part 3: Fruit That Remains

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 10, 2027.

JOHN 15:16 (ESV)

“I chose you and appointed you that you should go and bear fruit and that your fruit should abide.”

Much of life is measured by what can be seen quickly: numbers, attention, progress and results. Jesus measures differently. He speaks about fruit that remains.

In John 15, fruit grows from remaining in Christ, receiving the Father’s pruning and walking in love. It includes changed character, faithful obedience, answered prayer, love for others and lives influenced by the truth of Jesus. Some of that fruit becomes visible immediately. Much of it develops quietly over time.

This protects us from two dangers. The first is pride—thinking visible success proves we are spiritually healthy. The second is discouragement—thinking hidden faithfulness has no value because few people notice it.

A patient response to someone difficult may be fruit. Refusing an old temptation may be fruit. Teaching a child to pray, apologising sincerely, serving without recognition or speaking about Jesus with gentleness may all carry effects you cannot yet see.

The goal is not to appear impressive. It is to become faithful and useful in the hands of Christ. He chose His people to go into the world carrying His love and truth.

Do not spend your life producing what will disappear with the applause. Stay close to Jesus and ask Him to grow something that lasts.
