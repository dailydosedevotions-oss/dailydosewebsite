Words That Build, Part 2: A Gentle Answer

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 9, 2027.

PROVERBS 15:1 (ESV)

“A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.”

You cannot control another person’s tone. You can surrender yours.

A gentle answer is not weak or dishonest. It can be firm, clear and boundaried. Its strength is that it refuses to add unnecessary fire.

Harshness often feels powerful in the moment. It can silence someone, score a point or release pressure. But it rarely produces understanding. The words may last in another person long after our anger has passed.

Jesus could confront wrongdoing without losing control of Himself. His gentleness was not the absence of courage; it was power governed by holiness and love.

Before answering, lower the speed of the conversation. Pray if you can. Remove the insult, exaggeration and accusation. Say what is true without trying to wound.

Gentleness may not immediately change the other person. It keeps their anger from deciding who you become.
