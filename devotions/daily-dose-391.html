Faithful in Little

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 20, 2027.

LUKE 16:10 (ESV)

“One who is faithful in a very little is also faithful in much.”

We often imagine we would be faithful if the responsibility were larger, the opportunity better or the work more visible.

Jesus points to the little things. Character is already being formed in the private choice, the small promise, the modest amount of money and the task no one checks.

Greater responsibility does not automatically create faithfulness. It reveals the patterns already practised.

Do not wait for an impressive assignment. Answer honestly. Return what you borrowed. Keep the commitment. Handle the small resource carefully.

Big faithfulness is built from small obedience.
