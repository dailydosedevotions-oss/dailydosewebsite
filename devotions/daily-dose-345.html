Ask God for Wisdom

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 4, 2027.

JAMES 1:5 (ESV)

“If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach.”

There are moments when the honest answer is, “I do not know what to do.” Scripture does not treat that admission as failure. James tells us to ask God.

The wisdom described in the Bible is more than finding the easiest option. It means learning to see a situation in the light of who God is and what He has said. Sometimes wisdom leads to a clear answer. Sometimes it tells us to wait. At other times, it gives courage to do what is already clear but difficult.

God gives without reproach. He does not mock His children for needing help. But James also says we must ask in faith. That means coming ready to trust God’s answer, not merely looking for permission to do what we already decided.

Before collecting more opinions, slow down. Pray plainly. Search Scripture for commands and principles that apply. Seek counsel from mature believers who will tell you the truth rather than simply agree with you.

You may not receive every detail at once. Often God gives enough wisdom for the next obedient step.

Confusion is a reason to pray, not a reason to panic.
