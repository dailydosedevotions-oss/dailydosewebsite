Sanctified in the Truth

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 20, 2027.

JOHN 17:17 (ESV)

"Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth."

Jesus prayed for His people to be made holy. In JOHN 17:17 (ESV) He says, "Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth." Holiness is not produced by opinions, trends, feelings, or vague spirituality. God sanctifies through truth.

This matters in a world full of voices. Many things claim authority over the heart: culture, fear, trauma, desire, influencers, memory, and emotion. But Jesus names God's Word as truth. The believer must learn to bring every voice under the authority of Scripture.

Sanctification is not merely knowing more Bible facts. It is truth entering the life until desires, decisions, speech, priorities, and worship are reshaped. God's Word exposes lies, corrects compromise, comforts the weak, and trains the heart in righteousness.

PSALM 119:160 (ESV) says, "The sum of your word is truth." We do not get to choose only the parts that comfort us and ignore the parts that confront us. The whole Word is true.

Let Scripture have authority over you today. Not as information to admire from a distance, but as truth to obey, trust, and treasure. Jesus prayed for your holiness, and He named the means: the Word of God.

This should change how we approach the Bible. We do not come merely to collect comforting lines or prove our opinions. We come to be sanctified. That means Scripture may confront us, correct us, and expose what we would rather keep hidden. But that work is mercy. God's truth wounds the lie so the soul can be made clean.

Let the Word have the final say over feelings, culture, and private reasoning. If Jesus prayed for His people to be sanctified in truth, then truth is not optional for spiritual growth.

It is daily bread for holiness.
