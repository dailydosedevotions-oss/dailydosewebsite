Keep the Lord Before You

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 20, 2027.

PSALM 16:8 (ESV)

“I have set the LORD always before me; because he is at my right hand, I shall not be shaken.”

What stays in front of us shapes how we understand everything else.

If approval stays in front, rejection feels unbearable. If money stays in front, every decision becomes a calculation of gain and loss. If fear stays in front, even good opportunities can look dangerous.

David made a deliberate choice: “I have set the LORD always before me.” He brought God’s presence, character and promises into the centre of his attention. That did not remove opposition from his life. It gave him a steadier place from which to face it.

Setting the Lord before you may begin very simply. Read a short passage before reaching for your phone. Pray before entering the meeting. Ask what would honour God before asking what would impress people. Return to Him when distraction pulls you away.

For someone who is not yet sure what they believe, begin with Jesus. Read His words. Ask God, honestly, to show you who Christ is. Faith is not strengthened by avoiding the question.

You will still feel pressure. You may still be surprised by difficulty. But a life centred on God is less easily ruled by whatever shouts the loudest.

Place the Lord before the fear, before the decision and before yourself.
