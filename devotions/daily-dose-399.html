Finish the Race

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 28, 2027.

2 TIMOTHY 4:7 (ESV)

“I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.”

Paul wrote these words near the end of his life. He did not say the race had been easy. It had included rejection, prison, loss and suffering.

His confidence was not that he had built a comfortable life. He had kept the faith.

Finishing well is rarely one dramatic decision. It is built through ordinary days: returning after failure, refusing compromise, continuing in prayer and keeping Christ at the centre when other things compete for attention.

You may be near the beginning, exhausted in the middle or uncertain whether you can continue. Do not try to live the entire future today. Be faithful in this decision, this temptation and this opportunity.

A faithful finish is built one faithful day at a time.
