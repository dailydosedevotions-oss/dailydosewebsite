Commit Your Way

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 3, 2027.

PSALM 37:5 (ESV)

“Commit your way to the LORD; trust in him, and he will act.”

We often ask God to bless plans we have never truly placed in His hands. We want His help, but we still want control over the direction, timing and outcome.

To commit your way to the Lord means more than mentioning the plan in prayer. It means surrendering your right to demand that everything happen your way. It is trusting God enough to obey Him even when His path is slower, quieter or different from the one you imagined.

Psalm 37 was written for people tempted to become anxious when evil seemed to prosper. David repeatedly tells them not to fret, but to trust, do good and wait for the Lord. Commitment is not inactivity. It is faithful action without the burden of pretending to control the result.

Abraham had to leave without seeing the entire route. Israel had to follow the Lord one stage at a time through the wilderness. The disciples left their nets before they understood everything Jesus would ask of them. Again and again, faith moved forward because God was trustworthy, not because His people possessed every detail.

That is where commitment becomes difficult. We may surrender the beginning of the journey while secretly keeping ownership of the ending. We tell God we trust Him, but become angry when the timing changes or the door closes. True surrender lets Him choose both the road and the result.

What are you holding too tightly today? A relationship, decision, opportunity or future plan? Bring it plainly before God. Ask Him to close any road that would pull you away from Him and give you courage to walk the road that honours Him.

Then take the next faithful step. You do not need to force the whole future into place.

Place the way in His hands, and keep walking.
