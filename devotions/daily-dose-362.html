One Year of Daily Dose: Him We Proclaim

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 21, 2027.

COLOSSIANS 1:27–28 (ESV)

“Christ in you, the hope of glory. Him we proclaim.”

Today marks one year of Daily Dose. It is right to thank God for every reader reached and every opportunity to open His Word. But the anniversary should not leave our attention on a website, a number or the work behind it. It should turn our eyes again to Jesus.

Paul summarised his ministry in three words: “Him we proclaim.” He did not offer people vague inspiration or a method for becoming impressive. He proclaimed a Person—Jesus Christ.

Jesus is not merely one helpful teacher among many. Scripture presents Him as the image of the invisible God, the Creator and Sustainer of all things, and the Head of the church. Through His death on the cross, He reconciles sinners to God. Through His resurrection, He offers a living hope that death cannot defeat.

That is why Christian teaching must do more than provide useful advice. Advice may help us organise a day, improve a habit or manage a difficult conversation. Those things can matter, but they cannot forgive sin or bring us back to God. We need Christ.

Paul says, “Christ in you, the hope of glory.” Christian hope is not confidence that life will always become easier. It is confidence that those who belong to Jesus are no longer separated from God and will one day share in the glory of Christ. That hope can hold a person through suffering, uncertainty and even death.

If you have read Daily Dose for a year but have never personally trusted Jesus, do not stop at enjoying the encouragement. Look carefully at Christ. Read one of the Gospels. Consider His claims, His cross and His empty tomb. Ask God to show you the truth and bring your sin honestly to Him.

If you already follow Jesus, resist the temptation to move beyond the simple centre of faith. We never graduate from needing Christ. He remains our message, our hope and our life.

One year is worth recognising, but Jesus alone is worth proclaiming.
