Seeking With the Whole Heart

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 2, 2027.

JEREMIAH 29:13 (ESV)

"You will seek me and find me, when you seek me with all your heart."

Half-hearted seeking often wants the comfort of God without surrender to God. JEREMIAH 29:13 (ESV) says, "You will seek me and find me, when you seek me with all your heart." The Lord is not calling for a polished performance. He is calling for an undivided pursuit.

These words were spoken to people in exile. They remind us that even judgement and difficulty did not mean God was unreachable. The Lord was calling His people to seek Him truly, not casually, not as a religious habit with the heart somewhere else.

This exposes the way we can keep God at the edges. We may want guidance but not lordship, comfort but not correction, blessing but not holiness. Wholehearted seeking brings the whole life before Him: desires, motives, habits, relationships, plans, wounds, and secret places.

DEUTERONOMY 4:29 (ESV) says, "you will seek the LORD your God and you will find him, if you search after him with all your heart." This has always been the call: not surface religion, but a heart turned toward the Lord.

God is not hiding from the sincere seeker. But He will not be treated as one interest among many. Seek Him with the whole heart. Bring Him the divided places and ask Him to make them whole. The promise is full of mercy: you will seek Him and find Him.

Wholehearted seeking will often require letting go of the excuses that protect spiritual distance. It may mean turning off distractions, confessing sin, returning to prayer, opening Scripture with humility, or obeying what God has already made clear. The Lord is merciful to the seeker, but He is also worthy of the whole heart. Do not give Him the leftovers of your attention and call it pursuit.
