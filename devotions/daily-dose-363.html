Made New in Christ

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 22, 2027.

2 CORINTHIANS 5:17 (ESV)

“If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation.”

Many people wish they could begin again. We may try a new routine, move somewhere different or promise that this time we will change. Those steps can help, but they cannot by themselves make a person new at the deepest level.

Paul says new creation is found “in Christ.” This means being joined to Jesus by faith—trusting in His death for sin and His resurrection, and receiving a new identity from Him. Christianity is not self-improvement with religious language added. It is God giving spiritual life where sin had brought separation and death.

The past does not vanish. Consequences may remain, memories may still hurt and old habits may continue to fight for control. Yet they no longer have the right to define someone who belongs to Christ. Forgiven is now truer than condemned. Beloved is truer than abandoned. New does not mean finished, but it does mean the direction has changed.

If you are exploring faith, do not begin by trying to make yourself good enough for Jesus. Come honestly. He is the One who saves and makes new.

If you already follow Him, stop returning to an identity He has called you to leave behind. Walk today as someone who belongs to Christ.
