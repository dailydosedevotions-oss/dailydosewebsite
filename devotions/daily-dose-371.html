Go and Make Disciples

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 30, 2027.

MATTHEW 28:19–20 (ESV)

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations.”

Jesus did not command His followers merely to collect decisions, attract crowds or build religious audiences. He told them to make disciples—people who learn to trust, obey and follow Him.

That work includes going, baptising and teaching everything Jesus commanded. It is both an invitation into faith and a patient commitment to help people grow. A disciple is not produced in a single conversation.

The mission crosses every human boundary. “All nations” leaves no room for treating one culture, class or background as more worthy of the gospel than another. Jesus is Lord, and His good news is for every kind of person.

This calling belongs to the whole church, though it will look different in each life. Some go across borders. Others teach children, open their homes, support missionaries, explain Scripture, invite a friend or quietly walk beside a new believer.

The command is large, but it ends with a promise: Jesus is with His people always. The mission does not depend on our confidence alone. The One who sends us goes with us.

Ask where your next faithful step begins. It may be closer than you think.
