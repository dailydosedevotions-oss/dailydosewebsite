Life in the Spirit, Part 3: Power From Above

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 16, 2027.

ACTS 2:1–4 (ESV)

“And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit.”

At Pentecost, the disciples were together waiting as Jesus had commanded. Then the Holy Spirit came with the sound of a mighty wind, tongues like fire and speech in languages they had not learned.

This was not religious entertainment. God was announcing a new stage in His saving work. People from many nations heard His mighty works proclaimed in their own languages. The gospel was moving outward, just as Jesus promised.

Peter, who had once denied knowing Jesus, stood and explained what had happened. He spoke about Jesus crucified and risen, called people to repent and pointed them to forgiveness. The Spirit’s power made Christ known.

Peter connected the event to God’s promise through the prophet Joel: God would pour out His Spirit. Pentecost was not a sudden change of plan. It showed that what God had promised was now being fulfilled through the risen and exalted Jesus.

Pentecost also showed that God’s people would no longer be identified by one nation or language. Through the gospel, men and women from every background are brought into one people under one Lord.

Christians may disagree about some experiences and gifts of the Spirit, but the centre must remain clear. The Spirit glorifies Jesus, gives power for witness, creates holy lives and builds the church in truth and love.

Do not seek spiritual power to feel important. Ask God for courage to speak about Christ, strength to obey Him and love that crosses the boundaries people use to divide.

The fire of Pentecost was not given to make believers the centre. It was given to send them with the gospel.
