Destroying Arguments Against God

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 22, 2027.

2 CORINTHIANS 10:4-5 (ESV)

"We destroy arguments and every lofty opinion raised against the knowledge of God."

Paul is defending his apostolic ministry against opponents who judged by worldly standards. 2 CORINTHIANS 10:4-5 (ESV) says, "We destroy arguments and every lofty opinion raised against the knowledge of God, and take every thought captive to obey Christ."

The strongholds in view are not merely difficult feelings or every unwanted thought. They are proud arguments, claims, and patterns of reasoning that set themselves against the knowledge of God. Paul does not fight them with manipulation, force, or impressive human power. The weapons of gospel ministry have divine power because God's truth exposes what is false.

Taking every thought captive describes rebellious thinking being brought into obedience to Christ. The gospel confronts ideas that make human pride the judge of God and calls minds and lives to bow before the Lord Jesus.

This also teaches believers to test what they hear and believe. Familiarity does not make an argument true. Every teaching, cultural assumption, and cherished belief must be measured by God's Word and submitted to Christ.

PHILIPPIANS 4:8 (ESV) calls believers to think about what is true, honourable, just, pure, lovely, commendable, excellent, and worthy of praise. That supports disciplined, truthful thinking, but 2 Corinthians 10 should not be misused as a promise that Christians can effortlessly control every intrusive or unwanted mental event.

Do not be impressed by reasoning merely because it sounds confident or sophisticated. Ask whether it agrees with the truth God has revealed in Christ. The Lord does not fear proud arguments. His truth can expose lies, humble pride, and lead the mind into willing obedience.

Let every claim answer to Christ.
