EPHESIANS 2:10 (ESV)

“For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works.”

Just before this verse, Paul makes something clear: people are saved by God’s grace through faith, not by their own works. Christianity does not teach that enough good deeds can cancel sin or earn a place with God.

Then Paul immediately speaks about good works. We are not saved by them, but those who are saved are given a new purpose in them.

God’s “workmanship” means His work, not ours. In Christ, He forms people who increasingly reflect His character. Good works become the fruit of grace: generosity, honesty, courage, mercy, justice and service.

Many people wait for a dramatic calling while ignoring the need beside them. The work prepared for today may be a patient conversation, an apology, help freely given or an honest decision no one else sees.

Grace does not leave us with nothing to do. It frees us from trying to earn love and sends us to love others.
