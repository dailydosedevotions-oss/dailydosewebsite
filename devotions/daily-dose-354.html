A Renewed Mind

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 13, 2027.

ROMANS 12:2 (ESV)

“Be transformed by the renewal of your mind.”

Not every thought deserves to be believed.

Some thoughts come from fear. Others have been shaped by pride, past wounds, advertising, social pressure or repeated lies. A thought can feel familiar and still be false.

Paul says transformation involves the renewal of the mind. God changes how we live by teaching us to see what is true. Scripture confronts the belief that worth comes from achievement, that revenge brings peace, that desire defines what is right or that failure is beyond redemption.

Renewal takes time. It happens as truth is read, understood, remembered and obeyed. It also requires honesty. We cannot challenge a lie we refuse to name.

What thought has been ruling your emotions or decisions? Place it beside what God has said. If the thought says, “I must control everything,” Scripture reminds you that God is Lord. If it says, “I can never be forgiven,” the cross points to mercy for those who come to Christ. If it says, “My feelings must be obeyed,” God’s Word gives a wiser authority.

Do not simply ask God to change your circumstances. Ask Him to renew the mind you bring into them.
