Power, Love, and Self-Control

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 14, 2027.

2 TIMOTHY 1:7 (ESV)

"For God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control."

Fear can make obedience shrink. 2 TIMOTHY 1:7 (ESV) says, "for God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control." Paul writes to Timothy as one who needed courage for the calling before him.

This verse is not a promise that believers will never feel afraid. It is a reminder that fear is not our master. God has given what is needed for faithful obedience: power, love, and self-control. Not power without love. Not love without discipline. Not self-control without dependence on God.

Fear often scatters the heart. It imagines outcomes, magnifies opposition, and makes silence feel safer than faithfulness. But the gift of God steadies the believer. Power enables endurance. Love keeps courage from becoming harsh. Self-control keeps emotion from ruling the life.

ACTS 1:8 (ESV) says, "you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you." Christian courage is not personality. It is the Spirit's work in weak people.

Do not let fear write the boundaries of your obedience. Bring fear into the presence of God and ask for what He gives. Power. Love. Self-control. Grace does not always remove the challenge, but it strengthens the heart to obey within it.

This is not a verse for showing off courage. It is a verse for trembling servants who need God. Timothy needed stirring up, and so do we. The gift of God must not be buried under intimidation. If fear has made you silent, passive, or ashamed of obedience, let this Word call you back. God gives what fear cannot produce.

The Lord's gift is well balanced: power for obedience, love for people, and self-control for the inner life. Fear often distorts all three. Grace restores them so we can serve Christ faithfully.
