Ready to Give an Answer

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 28, 2027.

1 PETER 3:15 (ESV)

“Always being prepared to make a defense to anyone who asks you for a reason for the hope that is in you.”

Peter expected Christian hope to become noticeable. He also expected questions.

Being prepared does not mean having an answer to everything. It means understanding the foundation of your hope: who Jesus is, why His death and resurrection matter, and why you have trusted Him.

Peter adds that the answer should be given with gentleness and respect. Truth does not become stronger when it is delivered with arrogance. A person is not an argument to defeat; they are someone made in the image of God.

Think through your answer before the opportunity comes. Keep it honest. Do not exaggerate your past or pretend following Jesus removed every struggle. Explain the hope, not a polished image of yourself.

If you do not know the answer to a question, say so. Offer to look into it. Humility builds more trust than pretending.

Know what you believe. Know why it gives hope. Speak in a way that reflects the Christ you are describing.
