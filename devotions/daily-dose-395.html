Not by Might

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 24, 2027.

ZECHARIAH 4:6 (ESV)

“Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit, says the LORD of hosts.”

Zerubbabel faced a task larger than his resources: rebuilding the temple after years of ruin and opposition. God reminded him that the work would not be completed by human strength alone.

Ability, planning and effort are valuable, but they cannot replace dependence on God. This is especially true in spiritual work. We cannot argue someone into new life, manufacture holiness or build God’s kingdom through image and ambition.

Dependence is not an excuse for laziness. Zerubbabel still had to build. The difference was where he placed his confidence.

What are you trying to force through personality, pressure or control? Step back. Pray. Make sure the work agrees with God’s Word. Then act faithfully in the strength He provides.

God’s work must be done in God’s way and by God’s power.
