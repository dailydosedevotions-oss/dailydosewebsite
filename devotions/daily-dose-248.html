Number Our Days

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on December 28, 2026.

PSALM 90:12 (ESV)

"So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom."

The end of a year invites sober honesty. PSALM 90:12 (ESV) says, "So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom." Moses does not ask merely for better organization. He asks for wisdom that comes from rightly understanding the brevity of life before God. We do not naturally number our days well. We assume there will be more time. More time to obey. More time to repent. More time to forgive. More time to seek God seriously. More time to use gifts faithfully. More time to say what needs to be said and leave what needs to be left. But Scripture teaches us to live with reverence.

Numbering our days does not mean living in fear of time. It means living wisely because life is a gift and God is eternal. We are not ultimate. We are not guaranteed tomorrow. We are accountable to the Lord. Psalm 90 is honest about human frailty. Generations pass. Life is brief. Sin is serious. Yet the psalm also asks for God's mercy, gladness, favour, and established work. JAMES 4:14 (ESV) says, "you are a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes." That verse is not meant to make life meaningless. It is meant to humble presumption and lead us to depend on the Lord. As the year closes, do not only count achievements or disappointments. Number your days before God. Where do you need wisdom? Where have you delayed obedience? Where has time been spent on what cannot last? Where is God calling you to walk more faithfully? The wise heart does not waste conviction. Ask the Lord to teach you. Time is short, but mercy is real. Today can still be lived before Him. Numbering our days should not make us frantic; it should make us faithful. It teaches us to stop treating obedience as something to postpone until life feels easier. It teaches us to value people rightly, forgive sooner, repent honestly, and give ourselves to what will matter before God. EPHESIANS 5:15-16 (ESV) says to look carefully how we walk, making the best use of the time. Wisdom does not merely count days; it offers them back to the Lord.
