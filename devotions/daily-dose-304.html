He Will Restore, Confirm, Strengthen

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on February 22, 2027.

1 PETER 5:10 (ESV)

"The God of all grace... will himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you."

Suffering can make a person feel unfinished and unstable. 1 PETER 5:10 (ESV) speaks to believers after they have suffered a little while and says "the God of all grace... will himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you."

That title is beautiful: the God of all grace. Not a little grace. Not grace for only clean seasons. All grace. Grace for repentance, endurance, weakness, grief, obedience, warfare, and restoration.

Peter does not deny suffering. He does not pretend the Christian life is painless. But he places suffering inside the greater faithfulness of God. The Lord Himself will restore. He will confirm. He will strengthen. He will establish. The believer is not held together by circumstances behaving kindly.

PSALM 71:20-21 (ESV) says God will revive again and increase greatness and comfort. Scripture gives language for hope after deep trouble. The Lord can restore what suffering has shaken.

If you feel worn down, do not measure the future only by present weakness. The God of all grace is not finished. He can make the shaken steady. He can strengthen what has been exhausted. He can establish what feels fragile. After suffering, grace still speaks.

This promise gives courage to keep going when restoration feels slow. God may not rebuild everything in the way or timing we expect, but His grace is active. He knows how to strengthen faith through weakness, deepen roots through trial, and make His people steady after storms. Do not call the present struggle the final chapter. The God of all grace has the last word over His people.

Hold on to that when you feel unfinished. The Lord's work in you is not fragile. He can restore without rushing, strengthen without showing off, and establish you in ways that last.
