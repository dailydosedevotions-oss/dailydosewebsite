Discover His Goodness

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 18, 2027.

PSALM 34:8 (ESV)

“Oh, taste and see that the LORD is good!”

David does not merely tell people to discuss God’s goodness. He invites them to trust Him personally.

That invitation was not written from an easy life. David knew danger, fear and uncertainty. God’s goodness did not mean every circumstance felt good. It meant the Lord remained worthy of trust within them.

If you are unsure about God, bring your honest questions. Read one of the Gospels and look closely at Jesus—how He treated the broken, confronted hypocrisy, spoke truth and gave His life for sinners. Do not judge Him only by rumours, religious failures or painful experiences with people who used His name badly.

Taste and see. Take a real step toward the God Scripture reveals.
