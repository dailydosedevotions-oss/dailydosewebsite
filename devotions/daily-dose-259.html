Set Your Heart to the Word

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on January 8, 2027.

EZRA 7:10 (ESV)

"For Ezra had set his heart to study the Law of the LORD, and to do it and to teach his statutes and rules in Israel."

Ezra gives a needed order: study, do, teach. EZRA 7:10 (ESV) says he had set his heart to study the Law of the Lord, to do it, and to teach it. He did not treat Scripture as material for others while ignoring his own obedience.

That order matters. It is possible to know biblical language without a surrendered life. It is possible to explain truth while resisting it personally. It is possible to speak about holiness while avoiding the places where God's Word is confronting us. Ezra's heart was set not only to study, but to do.

Study is important because love for God does not mean carelessness with His Word. We should want to understand rightly. We should read with humility, patience, and reverence. But study that never becomes obedience can become pride. Knowledge must bow into faithfulness.

JAMES 3:1 (ESV) says that not many should become teachers, because those who teach will be judged with greater strictness. That does not mean truth should be hidden. It means handling God's Word is serious. Teaching should flow from reverence and obedience, not self-importance.

Set your heart to the Word. Not as a task to tick off, but as the path of life before God. Let Scripture examine you before you speak it to others. Let obedience be part of your understanding. A heart set on the Word is not only informed. It is being formed.

There is also patience in this kind of life. Setting the heart to the Word is not a one-week burst of attention. It is a direction. Some days understanding comes slowly. Some passages require careful study. Some truths must confront us before they comfort us. But a heart set on Scripture keeps coming back because it knows the Lord speaks through His Word. The goal is not to master the Bible as information, but to be mastered by the God who speaks.
