God Will Not Overlook

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 22, 2027.

HEBREWS 6:10 (ESV)

“For God is not unjust so as to overlook your work and the love that you have shown for his name.”

Being overlooked can make faithful service feel pointless.

People forget. They may enjoy what you provide without understanding the cost. They may notice one mistake and miss years of steady care.

Hebrews points to God’s justice. He does not overlook love shown in His name. That does not mean service earns salvation or places God in our debt. It means the God who saves by grace also sees what His grace produces.

If resentment is growing, bring it to Him. You may need rest, help or an honest conversation. But do not allow the absence of recognition to erase the value of love.

Serve from what God supplies, not from a desperate need to be praised.

What people miss, the Lord sees completely.
