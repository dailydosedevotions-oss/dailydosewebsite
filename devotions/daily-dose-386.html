Life in the Spirit, Part 2: Follow His Leading

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 15, 2027.

ROMANS 8:14 (ESV)

“For all who are led by the Spirit of God are sons of God.”

People sometimes describe every strong feeling or sudden thought as the Spirit’s leading. Scripture gives us a safer and deeper picture.

The Spirit inspired the Word of God, so He will never lead someone against it. He does not guide us into dishonesty, pride, sexual sin, revenge or confusion about Jesus. His leading produces obedience and increasingly puts sinful desires to death.

Romans 8 connects the Spirit’s leading with belonging to God as His children. This is relational, not merely directional. The Spirit does more than help us choose between options. He assures believers that they have been adopted through Christ and teaches them to cry, “Abba! Father!”

How, then, should we seek His leading? Read Scripture in context. Pray for wisdom. Pay attention to conviction. Seek counsel from mature believers. Examine motives. Then make decisions without demanding a supernatural sign for everything.

Sometimes the Spirit’s leading is less mysterious than we expect. Forgive. Tell the truth. Resist temptation. Serve. Speak about Jesus. Do the next thing God has already made clear.

Do not follow every impulse. Follow the Spirit in the path of truth and obedience.
