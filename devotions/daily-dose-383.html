The Fruit of the Spirit

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 12, 2027.

GALATIANS 5:22–23 (ESV)

“The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control.”

Character is revealed most clearly when life does not go our way.

Paul describes the character the Holy Spirit grows in those who belong to Jesus. The Holy Spirit is not an impersonal force. He is God present with and within His people, changing them from the inside.

Paul calls these qualities “fruit,” not decorations. Fruit grows from life, and growth takes time. We cannot tape patience onto an angry heart or manufacture peace through appearance. The Spirit changes desires, reactions and habits as we surrender to God.

This does not remove our responsibility. In the same chapter, Paul tells believers to walk by the Spirit and turn away from the destructive desires of the flesh—our fallen, self-centred nature. Growth involves daily choices, but those choices depend on God’s power rather than self-improvement alone.

Which fruit is most absent when you are under pressure? Do not merely promise to try harder. Bring the weakness to God, ask the Spirit to change you and choose the response that agrees with His work.

Spiritual maturity is not mainly how gifted someone appears. It is the character being formed when no one is impressed.
