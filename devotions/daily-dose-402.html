Do Not Forget His Benefits

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 31, 2027.

PSALM 103:1–2 (ESV)

“Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits.”

Forgetfulness feeds complaint. We can remember one disappointment in detail while overlooking months of ordinary mercy.

David speaks to his own soul. He deliberately remembers the God who forgives, heals, redeems, crowns with steadfast love and satisfies with good. His praise is grounded in God’s character and actions.

Remembering does not mean denying pain. Psalm 103 itself recognises human weakness: God knows our frame and remembers that we are dust. Gratitude and grief can exist together.

As May closes, look back. Where were you sustained? What did God correct, provide or teach? Who helped you? What prayer was answered differently from what you expected?

If you are still exploring faith, begin with the greatest claim of Christianity: God sent His Son to rescue sinners. Read the story of Jesus for yourself. Consider what His cross and resurrection reveal about both the seriousness of sin and the depth of God’s mercy.

Write down three mercies before the month ends. Thank God specifically. Carry those memories into whatever comes next.

Do not let familiarity make grace invisible.
