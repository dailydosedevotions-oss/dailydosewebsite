The Son Came to Serve

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 19, 2027.

MARK 10:45 (ESV)

“For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve.”

The disciples were arguing about greatness. Jesus answered by placing a servant at the centre—and then pointing to Himself.

Jesus had every right to honour, yet He chose humility. His service went far beyond helpful actions. He said He came “to give his life as a ransom for many.” At the cross, He gave Himself to rescue people enslaved by sin.

Christian service begins there. We do not serve to earn God’s acceptance or prove we are better than others. We serve because the King first served us.

That changes the tasks we consider beneath us. Washing feet was low work, yet Jesus did it. Listening, cleaning, caring, arriving early, staying late and helping without credit can all reflect Him.

Service also needs wisdom. Jesus did not satisfy every demand, and serving does not mean enabling harm or refusing all rest. It means our strength is no longer used only for ourselves.

Take the lower place today. Serve without announcing it. Let the action point beyond you to the Servant King.
