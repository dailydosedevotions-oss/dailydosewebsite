Daily Dose #184

October 25, 2026

TAKE COURAGE AND WORK

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on October 25, 2026.

HAGGAI 2:4 (ESV)

"Be strong, all you people of the land, declares the LORD. Work, for I am with you, declares the LORD of hosts."

God's people were called to rebuild, but discouragement was real.

The work before them did not look as glorious as what some remembered from the past. There were people who could compare the present rebuilding with former days and feel the weight of what seemed smaller, weaker, or less impressive. Into that moment, the Lord spoke through Haggai.

"Be strong... Work, for I am with you."

That is not empty motivation. God does not simply tell them to try harder because they are capable enough on their own. He anchors their courage in His presence. The command to work is held together with the promise that He is with them.

This matters because discouragement often grows through comparison.

We compare this season with a former season. We compare what we are building with what someone else seems to have. We compare the visible size of the work with what we imagined it would look like by now. We compare present obedience with past strength and wonder whether anything meaningful is happening.

But God does not call His people to be faithful only when the work feels impressive.

He calls them to obey because He is with them.

ZECHARIAH 4:10 (ESV) asks, "For whoever has despised the day of small things shall rejoice." Small does not mean abandoned. Beginning does not mean meaningless. Hidden rebuilding can still matter greatly before God.

There are assignments in life that require both courage and obedience. You may not feel ready. You may not see much fruit yet. You may be tired from starting again. You may feel like the work is too ordinary to matter or too difficult to finish.

But if God has given you work to do, His presence is the reason you can continue.

This does not mean every idea we have is automatically God's assignment. We must test our plans by Scripture, wisdom, prayer, and obedience. But when the Lord has made the next faithful step clear, discouragement must not be allowed to rule.

1 CORINTHIANS 15:58 (ESV) says to be "steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord."

That verse is anchored in resurrection hope. Because Christ is risen, labour in the Lord is not empty. Faithful work may be unseen by people, but it is not unseen by God.

So do not wait until every feeling lines up before obeying. Feelings may come later. Courage often grows as we move in faith, not before we take the first step.

The rebuilding in Haggai's day also reminds us that obedience can look unimpressive before it looks fruitful. Foundations are not always exciting. Repair work is not always visible. Clearing what has been neglected can feel slower than beginning something new. But God sees the faithful work that happens before the beauty is obvious.

NEHEMIAH 4:14 (ESV) says, "Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord, who is great and awesome." Nehemiah did not build courage by pretending opposition was unreal. He built courage by remembering the Lord. Fear shrinks the work until it looks bigger than God. Faith remembers who God is and returns to obedience.

There may be something the Lord has placed before you that needs steady hands again. A calling. A responsibility. A relationship that needs repair. A habit that needs rebuilding. A work that has been delayed because discouragement has been louder than faith.

PHILIPPIANS 2:13 (ESV) says, "for it is God who works in you, both to will and to work for his good pleasure." Even the willingness to keep going is grace. God is not only watching from a distance while you struggle to obey. He works in His people so they can work in faithfulness.

Take courage from His presence.

Put your hands to the work.

There is dignity in faithful work that people may never call impressive. The Lord does not need a crowd to make obedience meaningful. He does not need the work to look large before He calls it worthy of faithfulness. If He is with you, the assignment has weight.

PSALM 90:17 (ESV) says, "Let the favor of the Lord our God be upon us, and establish the work of our hands." That is a prayer for people who know their work needs God's hand upon it. We can labour, plan, build, and serve, but the Lord must establish what truly lasts.

So work with courage, but also with dependence. Lay the task before Him. Ask Him to purify your motives, strengthen your hands, and keep you faithful when progress feels slow.
