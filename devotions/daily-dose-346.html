Come and Rest

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 5, 2027.

MATTHEW 11:28–29 (ESV)

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”

Many people know what it is to be physically tired. There is also a deeper tiredness—the exhaustion of guilt, fear, pressure and always trying to prove that you are enough.

Jesus does not call weary people to pretend they are strong. He calls them to come.

The soul becomes exhausted from guilt, fear, striving and the pressure to prove itself. Christ’s rest is not freedom to live without Him; it is freedom from trying to carry life without Him.

Name the burden. Is it a responsibility God gave you, or pressure you placed on yourself? Bring it to Jesus and let Him correct both what you are carrying and how you are carrying it.

Real rest begins by coming to Christ.
