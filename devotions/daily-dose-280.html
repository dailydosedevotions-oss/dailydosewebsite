Set the Believers an Example

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on January 29, 2027.

1 TIMOTHY 4:12 (ESV)

"Set the believers an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity."

Example matters. Paul tells Timothy in 1 TIMOTHY 4:12 (ESV) to set believers an example in speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity. Timothy's age was not to become an excuse for spiritual carelessness. His life was to make truth visible.

This speaks to more than young leaders. Every believer influences someone. People see our words, reactions, priorities, relationships, consistency, and repentance. The question is not whether our lives say something. The question is what they are saying.

Paul names specific areas. Speech: what comes from the mouth. Conduct: how life is actually lived. Love: how others are treated. Faith: trust in God made visible. Purity: a life not surrendered to corruption. This is not image management. It is integrity.

TITUS 2:7 (ESV) says, "Show yourself in all respects to be a model of good works." Again, the point is not perfection for appearance. It is a life shaped by sound doctrine and sincere faith.

Do not wait until you feel important to live faithfully. Someone may be learning from your example now. Let your life point beyond yourself to Christ. In speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity, let the gospel have visible fruit.

This is especially important because example often speaks before explanation. People may forget many of our words, but they remember patterns. They notice whether confession is genuine, whether love is patient, whether purity is real, and whether faith remains steady under pressure. A godly example does not require pretending to be flawless. It requires a life that keeps returning to Christ and showing the fruit of His work.

The beauty of this is that God can use steady faithfulness in ordinary places. You may never know who is watching, learning, or being encouraged by your obedience. Let your example quietly make Christ look precious.
