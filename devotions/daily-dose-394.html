God Gives the Growth

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 23, 2027.

1 CORINTHIANS 3:6–7 (ESV)

“I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.”

Paul was addressing a church divided by personality. Some people claimed loyalty to him; others preferred Apollos. Paul refused the competition.

Both men had a role. One planted, another watered, but neither could create spiritual life. God gave the growth.

This truth removes pride when results are visible and despair when they are not. A parent can teach, a friend can speak, a pastor can preach and a believer can pray—but only God can open a heart.

Faithfulness still matters. Seeds should be planted carefully and watered patiently. We should explain the gospel clearly, live consistently and correct error. But manipulation is not ministry. Pressure cannot produce the work of God.

Perhaps you have been praying for someone for years. Do not confuse slow growth with useless faithfulness. Keep loving, keep praying and keep speaking truth when the door is open.

You are responsible for obedience, not for playing God in another person’s life.

Plant. Water. Trust the Lord with the growth.
