Daily Dose #268

Written and published by Daily Dose Devotions

January 17, 2027

Come Out From Among Them

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on January 17, 2027.

2 CORINTHIANS 6:16-18 (ESV)

"For we are the temple of the living God."

Belonging to God changes what we can join ourselves to. In 2 CORINTHIANS 6:16 (ESV), Paul says, "For we are the temple of the living God." That identity carries a call to separation from what defiles.

Separation is not spiritual arrogance. It is not pretending we are better than others. It is loyalty to the living God. The believer cannot treat compromise as harmless when God has called His people His temple. Fellowship with darkness is not maturity; it is danger.

This requires discernment. We are called to love people, witness faithfully, serve humbly, and live in the world without being of the world. But love for people does not require partnership with sin. Compassion does not mean agreement with what God calls unclean.

2 CORINTHIANS 7:1 (ESV) continues, calling believers to cleanse themselves from every defilement of body and spirit, bringing holiness to completion in the fear of God. The promise of God's fatherly nearness should lead to holiness, not casual compromise.

Ask where your loyalties have become mixed. What have you allowed close enough to shape your desires, dull conviction, or weaken obedience? The living God dwells with His people. Come out from what dishonours Him, not because you are proud, but because you are His.

This will sometimes require costly choices. There may be habits, entertainment, influences, partnerships, or environments that cannot remain as they are because they keep pulling the heart toward compromise. This is not a call to withdraw from unbelieving people whom we are to love and reach. It is a call to refuse participation in sin and binding partnerships that compromise loyalty to Christ. Separation does not have to be loud or self-righteous. Sometimes it is quiet obedience: stepping back, deleting, confessing, refusing, changing patterns, and choosing the Lord over what had been shaping us. The living God is worth that kind of clean break.

Holiness is never a loss when it brings the heart nearer to God. What sin calls freedom often becomes chains, but what God calls separation is part of His fatherly care. He does not call His people out to leave them empty; He calls them out to belong to Him more fully.
