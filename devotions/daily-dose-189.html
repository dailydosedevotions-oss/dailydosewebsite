Daily Dose #189

October 30, 2026

THE LORD WILL FULFIL HIS PURPOSE

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on October 30, 2026.

PSALM 138:8 (ESV)

"The LORD will fulfill his purpose for me; your steadfast love, O LORD, endures forever."

Psalm 138 gives courage to the heart that cannot see the whole picture.

David says, "The LORD will fulfill his purpose for me." That is not confidence in perfect circumstances. It is confidence in the faithful God whose steadfast love endures forever.

This matters because so much of life can feel unfinished.

Unanswered questions. Prayers still waiting. Growth still forming. Doors still unclear. Relationships still tender. Burdens still present. Obedience still costly. There are places where we want a completed story, but all we can see is an unfinished chapter.

The promise is not that every personal plan will unfold exactly as we imagined.

God's purpose is wiser, deeper, and holier than our preference. Sometimes His fulfilment includes refining, redirecting, waiting, pruning, and surprising grace. Sometimes He closes what we wanted open. Sometimes He opens what we feel too weak to enter. Sometimes He works slowly because He is doing more than we understood.

But the believer is not left to chance.

God is faithful in the unfinished places. He sees what concerns you. He knows what is still unresolved. He is able to complete what He has purposed according to His will.

PHILIPPIANS 1:6 (ESV) says, "He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ."

That is strong hope. God does not begin carelessly. He does not abandon what belongs to Him. His work in His people is not fragile.

But this hope also humbles us because it says His purpose, not merely our preference. Sometimes we ask God to fulfil our plan, when He is faithfully fulfilling His. The two are not always the same.

That does not mean His way is unkind.

Psalm 138 anchors the promise in steadfast love: "your steadfast love, O LORD, endures forever." God's purpose is not separated from His covenant love. He is not coldly arranging events without care for His people. His wisdom and love belong together.

Bring the unfinished parts to Him.

Bring the thing you do not yet understand.

Bring the place where the process feels slower than you wanted.

Bring the fear that too much is unresolved.

Ask Him to fulfil His purpose, not merely your plan.

ROMANS 8:28 (ESV) says that for those who love God, "all things work together for good, for those who are called according to his purpose." That verse is often quoted in painful places, and it must be handled carefully. It does not mean every event is good in itself. It does not make evil harmless or grief small. It means God is sovereign enough and faithful enough to work even through what we would never have chosen.

His good purpose is finally tied to making His people like Christ. The next verse speaks of being conformed to the image of His Son. That means God's purpose may reach deeper than the outcome we first wanted. He may be forming humility, endurance, holiness, compassion, faith, or surrender in the very place where we only asked for relief.

EPHESIANS 3:20 (ESV) reminds us that God "is able to do far more abundantly than all that we ask or think." He is not limited by our imagination, timing, or understanding. The unfinished parts of life are not too complicated for Him.

You can trust Him with what is not tied neatly yet. You can keep obeying while the story is still open. You can stop demanding that every unanswered thing must become clear before you believe He is faithful. His purpose does not depend on your ability to manage every outcome.

His steadfast love is strong enough for what is still unfinished.

This gives courage to keep obeying without needing to see the finished outcome. Faithfulness today is not wasted because God is faithful with the whole story. The work He is doing is deeper than the chapter you can currently read.

You may not understand the timing yet. You may not know why one door remains closed or why one prayer is still waiting. But you can know the character of the Lord. His steadfast love endures forever, and His purpose will not fail.

Rest the unfinished places in faithful hands.

The Lord is not late because you cannot see completion yet. His timing may be hidden, but His love is not. Keep your heart steady in the truth that His purpose is stronger than your uncertainty.
