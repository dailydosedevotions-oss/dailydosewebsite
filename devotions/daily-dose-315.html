He Satisfies the Longing Soul

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 5, 2027.

PSALM 107:9 (ESV)

"For he satisfies the longing soul, and the hungry soul he fills with good things."

The Lord knows the ache of the longing soul. PSALM 107:9 (ESV) says, "For he satisfies the longing soul, and the hungry soul he fills with good things." That is not a shallow promise that life will always feel easy. It is a testimony that God Himself meets the need beneath the need.

Psalm 107 tells of people in trouble crying to the Lord, and again and again He delivers. The pattern is honest: distress, cry, mercy, deliverance. God does not require the hungry soul to pretend fullness before coming. He fills the hungry with good things.

Many people try to silence longing rather than bring it to God. They distract it, indulge it, deny it, or bury it under busyness. But longing can become holy when it drives us to the Lord instead of away from Him.

ISAIAH 58:11 (ESV) says, "the LORD will guide you continually and satisfy your desire in scorched places." Scorched places are not beyond His care. Dry seasons can become places where God proves His sufficiency.

Bring the longing soul to Him. Bring the hunger you have tried to manage without Him. The Lord is not annoyed by need. He is the giver of good things, and His deepest gift is Himself. Nothing else can satisfy in the way He can.

This does not mean every longing is fulfilled immediately or exactly as we imagine. God often purifies desire while He satisfies the soul. He teaches us to hunger for what is good, lasting, and holy. Sometimes the first mercy is not getting what we wanted, but discovering that He Himself is enough to hold us, guide us, and fill what lesser things could only awaken.

That is why longing should not be wasted. Let it become prayer instead of complaint, surrender instead of escape. The hungry soul is safe with the Lord, because He knows how to fill without feeding what would harm us.
