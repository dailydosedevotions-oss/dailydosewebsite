Cheerful Giving

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on January 28, 2027.

2 CORINTHIANS 9:6-8 (ESV)

"God loves a cheerful giver."

Generosity is not meant to be driven by pressure or performance. 2 CORINTHIANS 9:7 (ESV) says each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for "God loves a cheerful giver."

This does not make giving casual. It makes it worshipful. The heart matters. A person can give outwardly while inwardly resenting it, performing for attention, or trying to purchase spiritual standing. God is not looking merely at the amount. He sees the heart.

Paul also connects generosity to trust. 2 CORINTHIANS 9:8 (ESV) says God is able to make all grace abound so that believers may abound in every good work. Generosity becomes difficult when we believe we are our own provider. It becomes freer when we trust the God who supplies.

This applies beyond money, though money is certainly included. Time, hospitality, attention, practical help, encouragement, and resources can all become ways love takes shape. But biblical generosity is not careless; it is faithful, thoughtful, and Godward.

ACTS 20:35 (ESV) records the words of the Lord Jesus: "It is more blessed to give than to receive." The kingdom turns our instincts around. We do not give to prove ourselves. We give because God has been generous to us in Christ.

Ask the Lord to loosen greed, fear, and self-protection. Cheerful giving is not forced emotion. It is a heart learning to trust the Giver.

Generosity also reveals what we believe about ownership. If everything is ultimately ours, giving will always feel like loss. But if what we have has been entrusted by God, giving becomes stewardship. The Lord does not call His people to reckless showmanship, but He does call them away from clenched fists. A cheerful giver has learned that God's provision is not threatened by obedience.
