Words That Build, Part 3: Guard the Door

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 10, 2027.

PSALM 141:3 (ESV)

“Set a guard, O LORD, over my mouth; keep watch over the door of my lips!”

David did not trust every word that wanted to leave his mouth. He asked God to stand guard.

Words can open doors to healing or damage. Gossip can make us feel connected while destroying someone who is absent. Exaggeration can win sympathy while moving us away from truth. A private frustration can become a public wound in seconds.

Guarding speech is not merely learning better manners. Jesus taught that words reveal what fills the heart. The mouth may expose resentment, pride or fear that needs God’s attention.

Ask three questions before speaking: Is it true? Is it mine to say? Can it be said in a way that honours God and the person involved?

Sometimes the right response is a courageous conversation. Sometimes it is silence. Wisdom knows the difference.

Invite God to guard the door. Not every thought needs an audience.
