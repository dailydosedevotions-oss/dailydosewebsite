Forgive as the Lord Forgave

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 17, 2027.

COLOSSIANS 3:13 (ESV)

“As the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive.”

Forgiveness is one of the clearest Christian commands and one of the easiest to misunderstand.

It does not mean calling evil good. It does not erase consequences, cancel the need for justice or require someone to remain in an unsafe situation. Trust may need to be rebuilt slowly, and reconciliation requires truth from both sides.

Forgiveness means releasing personal vengeance into God’s hands. It is refusing to make hatred your home. That can be a decision made before the emotions have caught up, and it may need to be repeated when the wound returns to mind.

Paul points to the forgiveness believers have received from the Lord. At the cross, God did not ignore sin. Jesus bore its cost. Christian forgiveness is therefore neither denial nor cheap sentiment. It begins with recognising how much mercy we ourselves need.

If you are new to faith, this command may sound impossible. In our own strength, it often is. Begin by telling God the truth about what happened and how it affected you. Ask Him to keep bitterness from becoming your master. Seek wise help if the wound is deep, ongoing or dangerous.

Forgiveness may not change the person who harmed you. It changes what you allow the harm to keep producing in you.

The road can be slow. Walk it with truth, wisdom and the mercy you have received in Christ.
