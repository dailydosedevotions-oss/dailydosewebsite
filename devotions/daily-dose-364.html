Let Your Light Shine

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 23, 2027.

MATTHEW 5:16 (ESV)

“Let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven.”

Jesus did not tell His followers to draw attention to themselves. He told them to live in a way that directs attention to God.

Light becomes visible through honest work, costly kindness, courage, mercy and integrity when compromise would be easier. Good works do not earn salvation, but they can show what God’s grace produces in a life.

There is a difference between being seen and needing to be praised. Jesus warns elsewhere against doing good merely to impress people. The aim is not, “Look how good I am.” It is, “Look what God is like.”

Serve someone today without turning it into a performance. Let the action be visible if it needs to be, but let the glory pass through you to the Father.

Shine clearly. Do not stand in the light asking everyone to notice the lamp.
