Written and published by Daily Dose Devotions

January 25, 2027

Deny Yourself and Follow

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on January 25, 2027.

MARK 8:34-35 (ESV)

"If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me."

Jesus never presented discipleship as self-improvement with religious language. In MARK 8:34 (ESV), He says, "If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me." That is a call to surrender.

Denying yourself does not mean hating yourself as someone made in the image of God. It means refusing self-rule. It means the old throne of "my way, my will, my comfort, my image, my control" must be surrendered to Christ.

Taking up the cross is not a small inconvenience. In Jesus' world, the cross meant death. The disciple follows a crucified Lord, and that means self-preservation cannot be the highest aim. We lose our life for Christ and the gospel, and in Him we find true life.

GALATIANS 2:20 (ESV) says, "I have been crucified with Christ." The Christian life is not built around protecting the old self. It is life by faith in the Son of God who loved us and gave Himself for us.

Where is Jesus confronting self-rule in you? Do not soften His words until they cost nothing. Deny yourself, take up your cross, and follow Him. The way is narrow, but Christ Himself is life.

This cuts across the message of the age. The world often says life is found in expressing yourself, protecting yourself, and fulfilling yourself. Jesus says life is found in losing your life for Him and the gospel. That does not make the disciple less human; it makes the disciple truly alive under the rightful Lord. Self-rule promises freedom but becomes bondage. Christ's call to surrender is costly, but it leads to life.

Following Jesus cannot remain an idea admired from a distance. It becomes real in decisions, appetites, relationships, money, forgiveness, purity, and obedience when no one is watching. The call is personal: deny yourself and follow Him today.
