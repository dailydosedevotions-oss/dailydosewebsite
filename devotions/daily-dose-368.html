Pray for the Harvest

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 27, 2027.

MATTHEW 9:36–38 (ESV)

“The harvest is plentiful, but the laborers are few.”

When Jesus looked at the crowds, He did not see interruptions. He saw people who were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd. His response was compassion.

That changes the way mission begins. It does not begin with irritation at people who think differently. It begins by seeing their confusion, pain and need through the compassionate eyes of Christ.

Jesus then told His disciples to pray for workers. The need was great, but His first instruction was prayer. Only God can open hearts, send workers and produce spiritual life.

Pray for the person whose name keeps coming to mind. Pray for your town, workplace, family and the places where hope feels absent. Ask God to send faithful people with the truth of Jesus.

Then remain available. Sometimes the person praying for workers becomes part of God’s answer. You may be the one who listens, invites, serves or speaks.

See the need. Ask the Lord. Be willing to go.
