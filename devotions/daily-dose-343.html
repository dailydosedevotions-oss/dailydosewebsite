New Mercy This Morning

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 2, 2027.

LAMENTATIONS 3:22–23 (ESV)

“The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning.”

Jeremiah wrote these words in the middle of grief, not comfort. Jerusalem had fallen, the people were suffering, and the future looked uncertain. Yet he deliberately called truth back to mind: the steadfast love of the Lord had not ended.

Perhaps you woke up carrying regret or disappointment. Bring it honestly to the Lord. His mercy is not permission to remain unchanged; it gives you room to repent—to turn from what is wrong—and begin again.

Confess what is yours to confess. Receive the mercy God gives through Christ. Do not live as though yesterday’s failure is stronger than today’s grace.

A new morning is another invitation to trust His mercy.
