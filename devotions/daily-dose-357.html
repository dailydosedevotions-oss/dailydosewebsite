Kind and Tenderhearted

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 16, 2027.

EPHESIANS 4:31–32 (ESV)

“Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you.”

Kindness is often mistaken for being agreeable, pleasant or unwilling to confront anything difficult. Biblical kindness is stronger than that. It grows from a heart changed by the mercy of Christ.

Paul places kindness beside a command to put away bitterness, anger, slander and malice. That means kindness is not merely a personality type. It is a deliberate rejection of the ways we wound others when pride takes control.

A tender heart still tells the truth, but it does not enjoy humiliating people. It can set a boundary without becoming cruel. It can disagree without treating another person as worthless.

For Christians, the pattern is God’s forgiveness in Christ. We did not receive mercy because we had earned it. Jesus bore the cost of sin and offers forgiveness to those who turn to Him. That mercy should reshape the way His people speak, respond and forgive.

Pay attention to the person in front of you today. They may be carrying something you cannot see. Let your words be honest, but do not let them be needlessly sharp.

Grace received should become grace given.
