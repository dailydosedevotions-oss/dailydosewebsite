Complete My Joy

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 28, 2027.

PHILIPPIANS 2:1-2 (ESV)

"Complete my joy by being of the same mind, having the same love."

Grace does not only reconcile us to God; it teaches us how to live with one another. PHILIPPIANS 2:1-2 (ESV) says, "complete my joy by being of the same mind, having the same love, being in full accord and of one mind."

Paul appeals to encouragement in Christ, comfort from love, participation in the Spirit, affection, and sympathy. Then he calls the church into unity. Christian unity is not built on pretending differences do not exist. It is built on shared life in Christ and humble love.

This challenges selfishness. Many conflicts grow because people protect preference, pride, control, or recognition. But the gospel creates a people who are learning to consider Christ and others, not only themselves.

JOHN 13:35 (ESV) says, "By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another." Love within the body is not a small matter. It bears witness to Christ.

Ask whether your presence strengthens unity or strains it. Are you carrying humility, patience, and sincere love? Grace does not make us passive about truth, but it does make us humble. Complete joy is found where Christ's people walk together in His love.

Unity is not built by demanding that everyone orbit our preferences. It grows when people who have received mercy begin to treat one another with mercy. The same Christ who humbled Himself for us teaches us to lay down pride for the good of others. Shared love is not weakness. It is evidence that grace is doing real work among us.

This begins in small choices: listening before answering, forgiving without keeping score, serving without needing attention, and refusing to divide over pride. The love of Christ makes unity more than an idea.
