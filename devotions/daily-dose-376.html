Doers of the Word

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 5, 2027.

JAMES 1:22 (ESV)

“Be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.”

It is possible to hear truth, agree with it and remain unchanged.

James says that can become self-deception. Familiarity with the Bible is not the same as obedience to it. A person may know verses about forgiveness while feeding bitterness, or speak about generosity while never giving.

God’s Word is a mirror. It shows us what is true about God and exposes what needs to change in us. Looking into a mirror is useful only if we respond to what we see.

Choose one clear instruction you already know. Do not search for something impressive. Make the call, tell the truth, stop the habit, offer the help or ask forgiveness.

Truth has not finished its work when it has only reached the ears.
