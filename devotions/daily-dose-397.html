God Is My Strength

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 26, 2027.

PSALM 73:26 (ESV)

“My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.”

The writer of Psalm 73 had struggled with envy, confusion and doubt. He watched arrogant people prosper and wondered whether faithfulness was worth it.

His perspective changed in the presence of God. He saw that temporary success was not the whole story and that having God was better than everything he envied.

“My portion” means God Himself is the believer’s lasting treasure. Bodies weaken. Emotions fluctuate. Money, health and human support can change. The Lord remains.

This is not a call to ignore physical or emotional limits. Rest, medical care and support may be necessary. It is a confession that none of those good gifts can carry the full weight of our hope.

When strength runs low, do not measure God by your energy. Tell Him the truth. Receive help. Hold to the One who does not fail when you do.
