Beauty for Ashes

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on March 27, 2027.

ISAIAH 61:1-3 (ESV)

"To give them a beautiful headdress instead of ashes, the oil of gladness instead of mourning."

The Lord's restoration reaches broken places. ISAIAH 61:1-3 (ESV) speaks of good news to the poor, liberty to captives, comfort for mourners, and "a beautiful headdress instead of ashes."

This passage is not vague inspiration. Jesus reads from Isaiah in His earthly ministry and identifies Himself with this anointed mission. The restoration promised by God finds its fulfilment in Christ. He brings good news, freedom, comfort, and righteousness.

Ashes speak of grief, ruin, and mourning. Many people carry ashes no one else sees: regret, loss, shame, disappointment, wounds, and years that feel wasted. Scripture does not mock mourning. It promises that the Lord is able to bring beauty where ashes seemed final.

LUKE 4:18-19 (ESV) records Jesus reading, "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me." Christ is not distant from the brokenhearted. He came with good news and liberty.

Do not assume ashes are the end of the story. Bring the broken place to the Lord. Restoration may not look like returning to what was lost, but Christ can bring life, righteousness, and praise out of what seemed ruined. He is the Savior who gives beauty for ashes.

This hope must be held carefully. God does not minimize grief, and neither should we. But grief is not stronger than the Anointed One. Jesus brings good news into places marked by captivity, mourning, and ruin. The same Lord who comforts also plants His people as oaks of righteousness, that He may be glorified.

The ashes may be real, but they are not beyond His redeeming hand. Let Christ bring His good news into the places you assumed were only ruined. His restoration carries glory.

He is not finished with the broken place.
