Run With Endurance

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 27, 2027.

HEBREWS 12:1–2 (ESV)

“Let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, looking to Jesus.”

Every runner learns that unnecessary weight becomes painful over distance.

Hebrews tells believers to lay aside both sin and “every weight.” Some things are clearly wrong and must be left behind. Other things may not be sinful in themselves but have become distractions, demands or attachments that keep us from faithful obedience.

The race is “set before us.” Comparison tempts us to run someone else’s course—to chase their pace, calling or appearance. Endurance grows when we accept the responsibilities God has actually given us.

Most importantly, the writer says to look to Jesus. Constantly staring at yourself produces either pride or discouragement. Jesus endured the cross, rose again and now sits at the right hand of God. He is both the source and the goal of faith.

What weight needs to be put down? It may be secret sin, resentment, endless distraction or the pressure to become someone God never asked you to be.

Lay it aside. Fix your attention on Christ. Take the next step in your own race.

Endurance grows by looking to Jesus, not constantly at the distance.
