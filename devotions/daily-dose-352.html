Jesus Does Not Change

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on April 11, 2027.

HEBREWS 13:8 (ESV)

“Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.”

Life can change in a single phone call. Health changes. Relationships shift. Plans fail. People who once felt dependable may no longer be present.

Hebrews points believers to a foundation that does not move: Jesus Christ remains the same. This does not mean He is inactive or distant. It means His character, authority and saving grace do not change with circumstances.

The Jesus who welcomed sinners still receives those who come to Him. The Jesus who spoke truth still refuses to flatter us. The Jesus who died and rose again still has power to forgive and give new life. He has not become less faithful because your world feels uncertain.

When everything changes, do not build your deepest confidence on what can change again. Bring your fear to Christ. Learn who He is from Scripture. Let His unchanging character steady your next step.

Unchanging Christ is steady ground.
