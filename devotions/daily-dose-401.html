The Lord Bless You and Keep You

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 30, 2027.

NUMBERS 6:24–26 (ESV)

“The LORD bless you and keep you; the LORD make his face to shine upon you and be gracious to you.”

God gave these words to Israel’s priests to speak over His people. The blessing is centred on the Lord: He blesses, keeps, shows grace and gives peace.

Biblical blessing is larger than comfort or financial success. The greatest gift is God’s gracious presence. A person can have an easy life and remain far from Him, or walk through difficulty while being deeply kept by Him.

The words also point forward to the grace revealed in Jesus. Through Christ, people who were separated from God can be forgiven and brought near. His peace is not positive thinking; it is reconciliation with God.

Receive the blessing without turning it into a promise that nothing painful will happen. Ask the Lord to keep your faith, make His character visible in you and give peace that circumstances cannot manufacture.

Then become someone who speaks blessing rather than harm over others.
