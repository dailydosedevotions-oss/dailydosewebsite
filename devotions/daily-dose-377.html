Known by Love

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 6, 2027.

JOHN 13:34–35 (ESV)

“By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.”

Jesus did not say His followers would be recognised mainly by branding, influence or the ability to win arguments. He pointed to love.

The love Jesus commanded is not vague niceness. He said, “Just as I have loved you.” Hours later, He would lay down His life. Christian love therefore serves, tells the truth, forgives, bears burdens and seeks another person’s good even when it costs something.

This love begins within the community of believers, where differences and disappointments make it real. A church can speak correct doctrine and still damage its witness through pride, cruelty or division. Truth and love must not be separated.

For someone looking at Christianity from the outside, the failures of Christians can be a serious barrier. Those failures should not be excused. Jesus remains the standard, and His people should be quick to repent when their behaviour contradicts Him.

Ask where love needs to become visible rather than merely spoken. Who needs patience, practical help or an honest apology?

The world should not have to guess whether following Jesus changes how we treat people.
