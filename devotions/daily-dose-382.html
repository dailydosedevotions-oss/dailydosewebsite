Honour in Complicated Families

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 11, 2027.

EPHESIANS 6:2 (ESV)

“Honor your father and mother.”

For some readers, this command brings warm memories. For others, family is connected with neglect, conflict or deep harm.

Honour does not require pretending wrong was right. It does not mean allowing abuse, removing necessary boundaries or obeying demands that oppose God. Truth and safety still matter.

At its heart, honour refuses contempt and revenge. Where the relationship is healthy, it may look like gratitude, time, care and respect. Where the relationship is broken, it may begin with prayer, truthful boundaries and a refusal to return cruelty for cruelty.

No family follows God’s design perfectly. That is why grace and repentance are needed on every side. Parents may need to admit harm. Adult children may need to release bitterness. Some situations require wise pastoral or professional help.

Ask God what honour looks like in the real circumstances—not the imaginary family you wish you had. Let obedience be guided by truth, wisdom and love.
