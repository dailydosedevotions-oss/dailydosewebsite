Commit Your Work

This devotion is scheduled and will become available at 7am on May 2, 2027.

PROVERBS 16:3 (ESV)

“Commit your work to the LORD, and your plans will be established.”

Committing work to God is not asking Him to approve every idea we have already chosen. It places the plan itself under His authority.

That means being willing for God to correct our motives, timing and methods. A goal can look good while being driven by pride, fear or the need to prove something. The Lord cares about both what we do and who we are becoming while we do it.

Pray before you begin. Ask whether the work is honest, loving and consistent with Scripture. Seek counsel where wisdom is needed. Then work wholeheartedly without treating success as something you can control.

If the plan succeeds, give God the glory. If it changes, remain teachable. If it fails, let the disappointment reveal where your identity had become attached to the outcome.

Commit the work, not merely the wish for success.
